SACRED MANTRAS

Mantras

Om Tat Purushaya Vidmahe,
Mahadevaya Dheemahi,
Tanno Rudra Prachodayaat

The following are some of the important mantras related to Lord Shiva. Chanting them daily, or at least on a frequent basis, is a sure way of obtaining Lord Somanatha’s blessings.

The following documents are in Kannada and have been reproduced here with the kind permission of Shaivam.org. We acknowledge with thanks the permission of Shaivam.org in letting us reproduce these documents here. Visit their website for more mantras in a wide variety of languages. The site is also a comprehensive portal for all information related to Lord Shiva.
