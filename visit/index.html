How to reach

There are four routes available to reach Nellitheertha.

Open in Maps

  1. From Mangalore, by the Moodabidri roadThe nearest city to Nellitheertha is Mangalore (about 360 km from Bangalore). You can find a direct bus to Nellitheertha from the Mangalore Central Bus Stand. Get on to the Moodabidri road and pass through Gurupura, Kaikamba and approach Yedapadavu. Just before you reach Yedapadavu, you need to deviate to the left and travel about 8 km to reach the temple.
  2. By the Mangalore to Udupi highway, through MulkiTake the Mangalore to Udupi highway and take a deviation to the right at Mulki. On that road, reach Kinnigoli and continue until you hit a V-intersection. Take the road to the left and travel 13 km to reach Nellitheertha (through Mucchur town).
  3. From Bajpe, through KathelsaarYou can also reach Nellitheertha from Bajpe via Kathelsaar. This would mean you need to take the Mangalore, Bajpe, Kathelsaar, Nellitheertha road. This is actually the shortest route to Nellitheertha from Mangalore and is about 14 km shorter than the Moodabidri route.
  4. From KateelNellitheertha is extremely close to Kateel, which is one of the most famous temples in South India. It is about 8 km from Kateel and can be reached via Ekkaru and Neerude.

Before you visit

  • The cave is open only between October and April.
  • All devotees who want to enter the cave bathe in Nagappa Kere first.
  • Any person above the age of 5 is allowed to enter the cave, irrespective of gender, belief or caste.
  • Inside, the trail narrows: one has to bend, then crawl flat on the tummy.
