BRAHMAKALASHA 2026

The invitation

|| Sri Somanatheshwara Prasannah ||

dhatriteerthaguheshwaram sukhakaram neerasya madhye sthitam |
vipraughashrutisamstutam smaraharam doshapaham shreekaram |
shreejabalimuneendrasevitapadam shreesomanatheshwaram |
bhaktabheeshtaphalapradam shivamayam kshetradhipam tam bhaje ||

Sri Somanatheshwara Guhalaya Sri Kshetra Nellitheertha
Kompadavu Village, Post: Neerude, Mangaluru - 574144 D. K.

Dear Bhagavad Bhaktas,

Starting on Swasti Sri Parabhava Nama Samvatsara Margashirsha Masa Shuddha Ekadashi, dated 20-12-2026, and culminating on Bahula Dwitiya, Friday, dated 25-12-2026, at 10:20 AM in the Kumbha Lagna Sumuhurtha, for Nellitheertha Sri Somanatheshwara Devaru,

Ashtabandha Brahmakalashabhisheka

With the divine blessings of the Honorary President of the Jeernoddhara Samiti,

Sri Sri Sri Vishwaprasanna Teertha Sripadangalu, Adhokshaja Pejavara Matha, Udupi

will be held under the leadership of the Kshetra’s Paryaya Tantri, Edapadavu Seethambettu Brahmasri Vishnumoorthy Tantri, and in collaboration with all the honorable Edapadavu Tantris.

And on the same night, in the divine presence of

Sri Sri Sri Vidyaprasanna Teertha Sripadangalu, Subramanya Matha, Kukke

by Sagri Sri Gopalakrishna Samaga Nagapatri and Sri Krishnaprasad Vaidya, Maddur, Dakke Balaga,

Sakrutpavitra Nagabrahma Mandalotsava

will be held.

Also, the Annual Jatre will be held from 25-12-2026 to 30-12-2026.

Therefore, we request you to actively participate in the Dharmika and Samskrutika programs to be held from 20-12-2026 to 30-12-2026, receive the Srimudi Gandhaprasada of the deity, cooperate in all ways possible, and become recipients of the complete grace of Sri Somanatha Devaru.
