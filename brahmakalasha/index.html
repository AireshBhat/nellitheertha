Special instructions

  1. Villagers belonging to the Nellitheertha Kudukattu are requested to bring and hand over their Hore Kanike items at 8:00 AM on 20-12-2026, the day of the Ugrana Muhurtha.
  2. Hore Kanike: Those who are offering Hore Kanike to Sri Devaru are requested to bring and hand over the offerings to the temple before evening on 20-12-2026, the day of Ugrana Muhurtha.
  3. Bhajana: Every day from 20-12-2026 to 30-12-2026, from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Bhajana programs will be conducted by various Bhajana Mandalis.
  4. Cultural Programs: On all days of the Mahotsava, every day from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM, various cultural art performance programs will be held.
  5. Since there is a requirement for Hingara and Bananas for the Nagabrahma Mandala Seve held on 25-12-2026, we request these two items in large numbers from the Bhaktas.
